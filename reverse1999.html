Resonance Tile Positioning

Maximizing the grid layout (Per Tile Max stats) for character blocks.
Grid Optimization Basics

Resonance layouts directly scale your character's baseline combat math. Shapes must be fit tightly together to maximize stat density per block.

Critical Focus

Prioritize L-shapes and cross tiles for raw Crit Rate and Crit DMG amplifiers. Ideal for main damage dealers like 37.

Survivability / DEF

Square blocks and long straight lines offer higher block efficiency for HP, Reality DEF, and Mental DEF scaling.

Quick Block Check

Always fill every tile in your grid. Leaving an empty slot reduces total passive bonuses by a significant margin.

Psychube Integration Guide

Matching Amplifications and basic stat lines to proper character kits.
Choosing a Psychube

Psychubes provide flat base attributes alongside a unique conditional Amplification skill. Always build around a character's specific action cycle.

Main DPS Requirements

Look for Psychubes offering Incantation DMG or Crit Rate / Crit DMG base traits. Amplifications should trigger easily on standard attack cards or Ultimate casts.

Support & Healer Roles

Prioritize Psychubes scaling with Healing Done, Damage Taken Reduction, or metrics that grant extra Moxie points to accelerate rotations.
